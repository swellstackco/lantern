## Shot notes
- Record the live check-in in one take with the mic; if ASR stumbles, use text input and keep the TTS — the drawer still proves it's real.
- Keep the Echo frame filling ~70% of the canvas; titles use the paper/ink palette; no stock footage.
- The day-38 vs day-70 beat is the whole argument — give it 8 full seconds of a still chart with the cursor.
- Say "fictional" once, early. Say "not a diagnosis" twice (middle and end).
- Research-claim lines (Penn State M2C2, WashU ARC, Rusz 2021 Parkinson's) are verified — do not add new claims while recording.
